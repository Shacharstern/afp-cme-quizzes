## High-yield clusters for Shlav A

2020 was a **COVID-era, broad–internal-medicine year** with heavy emergency/acute-care, GI, and guideline content.

**Acute care & emergencies.** Sepsis (pneumonia = most common source; 30 mL/kg crystalloid in 3 h; **norepinephrine** first vasopressor), ARDS (lung-protective 6 mL/kg PBW; older age a risk factor), eye emergencies (CRAO cherry-red spot; GCA → immediate steroids; Seidel test for globe injury), anaphylaxis (IM epinephrine; observe ≥6 h for biphasic), upper & lower GI bleeding (BUN/Cr >36 = upper; restrictive transfusion at 7; fecal calprotectin → IBD), wilderness medicine (HACE → descend; frostbite — don't rewarm if refreeze risk; mild hypothermia — insulate & rewarm).

**Cardiopulmonary & screening.** Acute chest pain (ACS = UA/STEMI/NSTEMI; rest-relieved pain + new ECG change → ED), office spirometry (low FEV₁/FVC = obstruction; normal ratio + low FVC → full PFTs), AAA (≥5.5 cm → repair), COPD exacerbations (antibiotics improve cure; CRP-guided care cuts antibiotics), AF anticoagulation (female sex alone doesn't tip a score-2 woman; mechanical valve → warfarin not DOAC).

**GI / hepatology.** NAFLD (obesity = top risk; no routine screening; continue statins), functional dyspepsia (<60 no alarm → test-and-treat H. pylori), C. difficile (negative GDH rules out; first recurrence → oral vancomycin; soap-and-water in outbreaks), chronic diarrhea (Rome IV onset ≥6 mo; weight loss = alarm), CRC screening (biennial FIT ≈ colonoscopy; FIT more positives → more colonoscopies).

**Women's health / peds / OB.** Shoulder dystocia (prior dystocia >8× recurrence; McRoberts first; PPH most common complication), ectopic pregnancy (rupture/instability → surgery; pre-MTX CBC/CMP/β-hCG), adolescent heavy menstrual bleeding (high-dose COC taper), ADHD (preschool → parent behavior training first), fever in infants (LP for all ≤1 month), GBS (well term infant, inadequate IAP → observe), pediatric migraine (CBT).

**Other recurring pearls.** Gout (allopurinol first-line; urate normal in acute flare; sugary drinks raise risk), tickborne disease (doxycycline for RMSF even in pregnancy; multiple EM = early disseminated Lyme), Paget disease (pain at night/rest; IV zoledronic acid; track with ALP), atopic dermatitis (varies by age; topical tacrolimus add-on), seasonal affective disorder (CBT; bupropion for prevention), Beers criteria (avoid cilostazol in HF), subclinical hypothyroidism (no levothyroxine if TSH <20).
