## High-yield themes that recurred in 2022

The year leaned heavily on **infectious disease/guideline updates** and **prevention**:

- **STIs / 2021 CDC guideline** appeared repeatedly (May Q12–14, Q20; Apr Q6–8) — anchor: ceftriaxone **500 mg IM** monotherapy for gonorrhea (1 g if ≥150 kg), add **doxycycline** when chlamydia not excluded, **NAAT** over culture.
- **USPSTF prevention** updates: lung cancer CT screening "**50–20–15**" (May Q7), AAA one-time screen for ever-smoking men 65–75 (Jan Q16), prediabetes (Jan Q21), pregnancy weight counseling for *all* pregnant patients (Feb Q21), dental fluoride varnish from first tooth (Mar Q21).
- **ACIP 2022 / pneumococcal**: PCV20 alone *or* PCV15→PPSV23 for adults ≥65 (Mar Q24).
- **Cardiology guideline shifts**: HCM now permits moderate-intensity recreational exercise (Feb Q24); ACG CRC screening — colonoscopy or annual **FIT** from age 45 (Mar Q25).
- **Antibiotic stewardship (ACP "shorter is better")**: 5-day doxycycline for uncomplicated CAP (Feb Q23).
