## Q1 — Caring for Transgender and Gender-Diverse Persons (p. 645)

Which one of the following statements about mental health diagnoses in transgender patients is correct?

- A. Most mental health concerns are secondary to being transgender.
- B. There is a high prevalence of traumatic life experiences in transgender and gender-diverse persons.
- C. Transgender and gender-diverse patients with mental health diagnoses should always be referred to a subspecialist.
- D. Efforts to convert gender identity to align with sex assigned at birth are appropriate and often effective.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — There is a high prevalence of traumatic life experiences in transgender/gender-diverse persons.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Distress largely stems from **stigma/minority stress and trauma**, not from being transgender per se (A); most can be managed in primary care (C); **"conversion" efforts are harmful and ineffective** (D wrong).

## Q2 — Caring for Transgender and Gender-Diverse Persons (p. 645)

Which one of the following statements about preventive services for transgender and gender-diverse persons is correct?

- A. Standard screening recommendations for hyperlipidemia, diabetes mellitus, hypertension, and obesity are rarely appropriate.
- B. Screening for osteoporosis is not based on hormone use.
- C. Transgender females with breast tissue and transgender males who have not undergone complete mastectomy do not require mammography to screen for breast cancer.
- D. Cervical and prostate cancer screening should be based on the presence of relevant anatomy.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Screen for cervical/prostate cancer based on the organs the patient has ("anatomic inventory").** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Standard cardiometabolic screening still applies (A wrong); osteoporosis risk **is** influenced by hormone therapy (B wrong); patients with breast tissue **do** need breast cancer screening (C wrong).

## Q3 — Caring for Transgender and Gender-Diverse Persons (p. 645)

Which one of the following statements about transgender and gender-diverse youth is correct?

- A. It is unusual for questions about gender identity to date back to childhood.
- B. The family environment does not have a clear impact on resilience or psychological outcomes.
- C. Transgender and gender-diverse adolescents should have access to psychological therapy for support and a safe means to explore their gender identity, and to adjust to socioemotional aspects of gender incongruence.
- D. Delaying puberty suppression and subsequent gender-affirming hormone therapy does not increase the risk of psychiatric stress or gender-related abuse.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Adolescents should have access to supportive psychological therapy to safely explore gender identity.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Gender questions often **do** date to childhood (A wrong); **family support strongly affects** outcomes/resilience (B wrong); delaying care can **increase** distress (D wrong).

## Q4 — Dental Problems in Primary Care (p. 654)

A 36-year-old man who has not seen a dentist for several years presents with signs and symptoms consistent with periodontitis. Which one of the following is an appropriate initial treatment?

- A. Oral antibiotics.
- B. Use of chlorhexidine (Peridex) oral rinse.
- C. Scaling and root planing.
- D. Removing trapped debris.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Scaling and root planing (by a dental professional).** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Mechanical removal of subgingival plaque/calculus is the foundation of periodontitis treatment. Chlorhexidine and antibiotics are **adjuncts**, not the primary therapy (A, B).

## Q5 — Dental Problems in Primary Care (p. 654)

Which one of the following is the preferred treatment for avulsed permanent teeth?

- A. Splinting.
- B. Immediate reimplantation.
- C. Reimplantation within 48 hours.
- D. Root canal.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Immediate reimplantation.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Reimplant an avulsed **permanent** tooth as fast as possible (ideally within minutes); survival drops sharply with delay. If immediate reimplantation isn't possible, store in milk/saline/saliva. (Avulsed **primary** teeth are not reimplanted.)

## Q6 — Dental Problems in Primary Care (p. 654)

How much fluoride toothpaste should a four-year-old child use for brushing?

- A. None; fluoride toothpaste should not be used until six years of age.
- B. A smear.
- C. A pea-sized amount.
- D. The same amount as for adults.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — A pea-sized amount.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Use a **smear (rice-grain)** under age 3 and a **pea-sized** amount for ages 3–6. Fluoride toothpaste is recommended from tooth eruption (A wrong).

## Q7 — Crohn's Disease: Diagnosis and Management (p. 661)

Which one of the following is **not** considered a red flag for diagnosing Crohn's disease in adults?

- A. Weight loss in the past three months.
- B. Nocturnal diarrhea.
- C. Family history of inflammatory bowel disease in a first-degree relative.
- D. Hemorrhoids.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Hemorrhoids.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Red flags include weight loss, **nocturnal/bloody** diarrhea, and a family history of IBD. Hemorrhoids are common and nonspecific (perianal **fistulas/abscesses/skin tags** would be the Crohn-suggestive findings).

## Q8 — Crohn's Disease (p. 661)

Which one of the following tests is reasonable for ruling out Crohn's disease?

- A. C-reactive protein.
- B. Stool studies for Clostridium difficile.
- C. Fecal calprotectin.
- D. Stool lactoferrin.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Fecal calprotectin.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> A normal **fecal calprotectin** (a sensitive marker of intestinal inflammation) helps **exclude** IBD and avoid unnecessary endoscopy. CRP is less specific; C. difficile testing addresses infection, not Crohn's (A, B).

## Q9 — Crohn's Disease (p. 661)

Which one of the following should be performed initially in a toxic-appearing patient with suspected Crohn's disease?

- A. Ileocolonoscopy.
- B. Cross-sectional imaging.
- C. Capsule endoscopy.
- D. Standard computed tomography.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Standard computed tomography.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: Medium ⚠️
> In a **toxic-appearing** patient, urgent CT rapidly evaluates for complications (obstruction, perforation, abscess, toxic megacolon). **Colonoscopy** (perforation risk) and **capsule endoscopy** (retention risk) are contraindicated acutely (A, C). *(Option B "cross-sectional imaging" is the general category that includes CT; the key selects standard CT.)*

## Q10 — Cochrane for Clinicians: Deep Venous Thrombosis — Home vs. Inpatient Treatment (p. 642)

Which one of the following statements about the treatment of deep venous thrombosis in the inpatient setting vs. the outpatient setting is correct?

- A. At-home treatment with low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) is associated with an increased risk of recurrence of venous thromboembolism.
- B. At-home treatment with LMWH is not associated with an increased risk of bleeding.
- C. At-home treatment with LMWH is no more cost-effective than inpatient treatment.
- D. All of the above.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Home LMWH treatment does not increase bleeding risk.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Outpatient DVT treatment is safe — **no increase** in recurrence (A wrong) or bleeding, and it's **more** cost-effective (C wrong) for appropriately selected patients.

## Q11 — Cochrane for Clinicians: Preventing or Delaying Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus with Diet and Exercise (p. 643)

Which one of the following statements about the use of diet and exercise to treat a patient with prediabetes is correct?

- A. Evidence shows that diet together with exercise affects the incidence of type 2 diabetes.
- B. Implementing changes in diet alone can prevent type 2 diabetes.
- C. An increase in physical activity with no changes in diet prevents progression to type 2 diabetes.
- D. All of the above.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — Combined diet and exercise reduces the incidence of type 2 diabetes.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The strongest evidence is for **diet plus exercise together**. Diet alone or exercise alone individually lacked the same demonstrated effect in this review (B, C), so "all of the above" is wrong (D).

## Q12 — Practice Guidelines: Lung Cancer Screening Recommendations from the ACCP (p. 688)

Which one of the following patients should receive lung cancer screening with computed tomography, according to the American College of Chest Physicians (ACCP)?

- A. A 53-year-old man with a 26-pack-year smoking history who is classified as high risk on a clinical prediction calculator.
- B. A 68-year-old woman with a 30-pack-year smoking history who quit smoking 18 years ago.
- C. A 75-year-old man with a 31-pack-year smoking history and advanced liver disease.
- D. A 66-year-old woman with a 32-pack-year smoking history and well-controlled diabetes.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — The 66-year-old woman (32 pack-years, no life-limiting comorbidity).** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> She meets criteria: appropriate **age (55–77), ≥30 pack-years, recent/current smoking, no comorbidity that limits benefit**. The others fail on age/pack-years (<55, <30 — A), **quit >15 years ago** (B), or a **life-limiting comorbidity** (advanced liver disease — C).
