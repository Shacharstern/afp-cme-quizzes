# AFP CME Quiz — August 15, 2018 (Vol 98, Issue 4)

> [!info] Source & answer provenance

## Q1 — Bone Cancer: Diagnosis and Treatment Principles (p. 205)

Which one of the following is the preferred imaging modality to diagnose malignant bone tumors?

- A. Magnetic resonance imaging.
- B. Bone scan.
- C. Plain radiography.
- D. Positron emission tomography.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Plain radiography.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Plain films are the **first** and most useful study for characterizing a bone lesion (aggressive features). MRI is then used for local staging; bone scan/PET assess metastatic spread.

## Q2 — Bone Cancer (p. 205)

Which one of the following therapies is generally effective for Ewing sarcoma but not osteosarcoma?

- A. Surgery.
- B. Radiation therapy.
- C. Neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
- D. Adjuvant chemotherapy.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Radiation therapy.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> **Ewing sarcoma is radiosensitive**; osteosarcoma is relatively radioresistant (managed with surgery + chemotherapy). Both use chemotherapy and surgery.

## Q3 — Bone Cancer (p. 205)

Which one of the following is a poor prognostic factor for its respective bone cancer?

- A. Older age in a patient with osteosarcoma.
- B. 95% response to chemotherapy in a patient with Ewing sarcoma.
- C. Localized extremity disease in a patient with osteosarcoma.
- D. Grade 1 tumor in a patient with chondrosarcoma.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — Older age in osteosarcoma.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Older age worsens osteosarcoma prognosis. Good chemotherapy response, localized extremity disease, and a low-grade chondrosarcoma are all **favorable** features (B, C, D).

## Q4 — Preoperative Assessment in Older Adults (p. 214)

A 70-year-old man with a history of depression is suspected of having impaired cognition perioperatively. Which one of the following statements is correct?

- A. Surgical outcomes are the same regardless of cognitive function.
- B. Functional status should play a minimal role in determining whether the patient is a surgical candidate.
- C. The patient is not a candidate for the Aid to Capacity Evaluation tool.
- D. The patient is at risk of postoperative delirium.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — He is at risk of postoperative delirium.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Pre-existing cognitive impairment (and depression) predict postoperative delirium. Cognitive and **functional status strongly affect** outcomes/candidacy (A, B wrong); capacity tools can still be applied (C wrong).

## Q5 — Preoperative Assessment in Older Adults (p. 214)

Which one of the following domains is associated with postoperative wound dehiscence in older patients?

- A. Impaired cognition.
- B. Malnutrition.
- C. High fall risk.
- D. Smoking.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Malnutrition.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Poor nutritional status impairs wound healing → dehiscence. (Smoking also harms healing, but among these *geriatric-assessment domains* the article links **malnutrition** to dehiscence.)

## Q6 — Preoperative Assessment in Older Adults (p. 214)

Which one of the following statements on reducing the risk of postoperative complications in older patients is correct?

- A. Hearing aids and glasses may reduce the risk of delirium.
- B. Patients should cut back on exercise regimens two weeks before surgery.
- C. Smoking cessation can worsen postoperative physical functioning.
- D. Postoperative nutritional supplementation slows recovery.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — Providing hearing aids and glasses may reduce delirium risk.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Sensory aids are part of multicomponent delirium prevention. **Prehabilitation/exercise** is encouraged (B wrong); smoking cessation and nutritional support **improve** recovery (C, D wrong).

## Q7 — Nutrition in Toddlers (p. 227)

Which one of the following statements about beverage consumption in toddlers is correct?

- A. In general, children should be transitioned to reduced-fat cow's milk at one year of age.
- B. Almond milk is the preferred alternative to cow's milk because of its nutritional similarity.
- C. In children one to three years of age, 100% fruit juice should be limited to 8 oz per day.
- D. Water is the best option to offer toddlers between meals.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Water is the best between-meal beverage for toddlers.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Transition to **whole** milk at age 1 (reduced-fat later, A wrong); almond milk is **nutritionally inferior** (low protein/fat, B wrong); juice should be limited to **~4 oz/day** at 1–3 years (C wrong).

## Q8 — Nutrition in Toddlers (p. 227)

Which one of the following statements about a toddler's diet is correct?

- A. Carbohydrates should comprise the largest percentage of macronutrients in a toddler's diet.
- B. Iron deficiency is best prevented by increasing intake of cow's milk to 1 L per day.
- C. Iron supplementation is recommended for all children six months to two years of age.
- D. A daily multivitamin is recommended for most toddlers.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — Carbohydrates should be the largest macronutrient share.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Excess cow's milk **causes** iron deficiency (limit to ~16–24 oz/day, B wrong); routine iron supplementation isn't for all infants/toddlers (C wrong); most toddlers eating a varied diet don't need a multivitamin (D wrong).

## Q9 — Nutrition in Toddlers (p. 227)

Which one of the following behavioral interventions is recommended to reduce picky eating in children?

- A. Using foods to soothe a child.
- B. Reducing the number of times a child is offered new foods.
- C. Pushing the child to finish everything on his or her plate.
- D. Pairing new foods with familiar foods.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Pair new foods with familiar foods.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Repeated, pressure-free exposure (often pairing new with accepted foods) reduces picky eating. **Don't** use food as comfort (A), reduce exposures (B), or pressure plate-clearing (C).

## Q10 — Epistaxis: Outpatient Management (p. 240)

Which one of the following statements about anterior epistaxis is correct?

- A. It accounts for a minority of epistaxis cases.
- B. It typically occurs along the nasal septum or lateral nasal wall.
- C. It commonly occurs from the Kiesselbach plexus.
- D. Hemostasis is more difficult to achieve for anterior epistaxis than for posterior epistaxis.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — It commonly arises from the Kiesselbach plexus (anterior septum).** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Anterior bleeds are the **majority** (A wrong), arise from the anterior **septum** (B imprecise), and are **easier** to control than posterior bleeds (D reversed).

## Q11 — Epistaxis (p. 240)

Which one of the following is the appropriate stepwise order of interventions in a patient with anterior epistaxis?

- A. Directive therapy, compressive therapy, nasal packing.
- B. Compressive therapy, directive therapy, nasal packing.
- C. Compressive therapy, nasal packing, directive therapy.
- D. Nasal packing, compressive therapy, directive therapy.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Compressive therapy → directive therapy (cautery/vasoconstrictors) → nasal packing.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Start by **pinching/compressing** the nostrils, then targeted (directive) measures like topical vasoconstrictors/cautery, and only then **packing** if bleeding persists.

## Q12 — Epistaxis (p. 240)

Which one of the following statements about posterior epistaxis is correct?

- A. Patients with posterior epistaxis are less likely to require hospitalization than those with anterior epistaxis.
- B. Chemical cautery is often useful.
- C. Nasal packing is up to 90% effective at stopping bleeding.
- D. Endoscopic artery ligation is the best initial treatment.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Endoscopic arterial ligation is favored as an effective treatment for posterior epistaxis.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: Medium ⚠️
> Posterior bleeds **more often** need hospitalization (A wrong), are hard to **cauterize chemically** (B wrong), and posterior packing is **less** effective/less comfortable than the ~90% figure for anterior bleeds (C overstated). *(Teaching nuance: posterior packing is still a common first step; the article/key favors early endoscopic ligation.)*

## Q13 — Putting Prevention into Practice: Interventions to Prevent Falls and Fractures in Community-Dwelling Older Adults (p. 253)

A 75-year-old man who recently fell presents for his annual wellness visit. According to the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, which one of the following interventions should be recommended because it has been found to have a moderate benefit in preventing falls?

- A. Education on falls prevention.
- B. Group exercise classes three times per week.
- C. Cognitive behavior therapy.
- D. Environmental assessment of the patient's home.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Exercise interventions (e.g., group exercise classes).** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The USPSTF recommends **exercise** to prevent falls in community-dwelling adults ≥65 at increased risk (moderate benefit). Education and CBT aren't fall-prevention interventions (A, C); multifactorial/environmental interventions are **selective/individualized** (D).

## Q14 — Point-of-Care Guides: Identifying Patients with Headache Who Are at Risk of Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (p. 256)

Which one of the following statements about the Ottawa Subarachnoid Hemorrhage clinical decision rule in patients with headache is correct?

- A. It applies to patients 13 years and older.
- B. It should be used in patients with a history of trauma.
- C. Presence of chronic headaches warrants imaging.
- D. Presence of neck pain is a risk factor that warrants imaging.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Neck pain/stiffness is a rule criterion that warrants investigation.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The Ottawa SAH rule applies to alert patients **≥15** with new acute **atraumatic** headache peaking within 1 hour (A, B wrong) and **excludes** those with recurrent/chronic headaches (C wrong). Its criteria include neck pain/stiffness, age ≥40, witnessed LOC, exertional onset, thunderclap, and limited neck flexion.

## Q15 — Practice Guidelines: Safe Reduction of Primary Cesarean Births — National Partnership for Maternal Safety (p. 258)

Which one of the following statements about strategies to reduce cesarean births is correct?

- A. Research shows that women who are admitted presenting in latent labor have lower rates of cesarean birth.
- B. Suppressive therapy for herpes simplex virus is a contraindication to vaginal delivery.
- C. Redefining active labor as dilation of 6 cm rather than 4 cm is one strategy studied to reduce the chances of a cesarean birth.
- D. Continuous electronic fetal monitoring is recommended to prevent cesarean section in uncomplicated, nulliparous, term pregnancies in spontaneous labor.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Redefining active labor as 6 cm (rather than 4 cm) helps reduce cesarean births.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Admitting in **latent** labor **increases** cesareans (A reversed); HSV suppressive therapy **enables** vaginal delivery (B wrong); **intermittent auscultation** (not continuous EFM) is preferred in low-risk labor (D reversed).
