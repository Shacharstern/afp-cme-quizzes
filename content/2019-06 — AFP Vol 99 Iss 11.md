## Q1 — Varicose Veins: Diagnosis and Treatment (p. 682)

Which one of the following findings in the evaluation of varicose veins is an early sign of advanced venous disease?

- A. Throbbing.
- B. Hemodynamically significant external hemorrhage.
- C. Corona phlebectatica.
- D. Relief with leg elevation.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Corona phlebectatica.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> A fan of small intradermal veins at the medial ankle (**corona phlebectatica / "ankle flare"**) is an early marker of advanced chronic venous disease. Throbbing and relief with elevation are common nonspecific symptoms; external hemorrhage is a late complication.

## Q2 — Varicose Veins (p. 682)

Which one of the following imaging modalities is the best way to evaluate varicose veins in preparation for an interventional procedure?

- A. Venography.
- B. Venous duplex ultrasonography.
- C. Plethysmography.
- D. Magnetic resonance imaging.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Venous duplex ultrasonography.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Duplex ultrasound maps **reflux and anatomy** noninvasively and is the standard pre-procedure study. Venography is invasive/reserved for complex cases; plethysmography and MRI are adjuncts.

## Q3 — Varicose Veins (p. 682)

Which one of the following statements about the treatment of varicose veins is correct?

- A. Endovenous thermal ablation should be considered only after a trial of conservative treatment.
- B. Surgery is considered the best interventional treatment option.
- C. Sclerotherapy is better at maintaining saphenous vein occlusion at six months than endovenous laser ablation or surgery.
- D. Endovenous thermal ablation is considered first-line therapy for nonpregnant patients.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Endovenous thermal ablation is first-line interventional therapy for nonpregnant patients.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Endovenous ablation has largely replaced surgery (less morbidity, B wrong) and need not wait for a conservative trial (A wrong). **Sclerotherapy has lower durable occlusion** than thermal ablation/surgery (C reversed).

## Q4 — Adolescent Substance Use and Misuse: Recognition and Management (p. 689)

Which one of the following statements about adolescent substance use is correct?

- A. Traditional cigarette use is rising.
- B. Marijuana is the most commonly used substance.
- C. Hospitalization from prescription opioid poisoning is rising.
- D. Adolescent males have higher rates of nonmedical use of amphetamines and tranquilizers than adolescent females.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Hospitalizations from prescription opioid poisoning are rising.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Traditional cigarette use is **declining** (replaced by e-cigarettes, A); **alcohol** remains the most commonly used substance (B); adolescent **females** have higher nonmedical amphetamine/tranquilizer use (D reversed).

## Q5 — Adolescent Substance Use and Misuse (p. 689)

What is the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force and the American Academy of Family Physicians recommendation for primary care–based behavioral interventions to prevent or reduce illicit substance use or nonmedical pharmaceutical use in children or adolescents?

- A. Routinely recommended.
- B. Selectively recommended.
- C. Recommended against.
- D. Insufficient evidence to determine harms and benefits.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **D — Insufficient evidence (I statement).** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The USPSTF concluded there is **insufficient evidence** to assess the benefits/harms of primary care behavioral interventions for illicit/nonmedical drug use in youth.

## Q6 — Adolescent Substance Use and Misuse (p. 689)

Which one of the following statements about the split-visit model is correct?

- A. Parents participate in the clinical visit for a limited time and then leave the room.
- B. Parents do not participate in the clinical visit.
- C. Parents stay in the room with the adolescent the whole time during the clinical visit.
- D. The American Academy of Family Physicians discourages adolescent-physician confidential visits.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — Parents join for part of the visit, then leave so the adolescent can be seen confidentially.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The split visit balances parental input with **confidential one-on-one time** — which the AAFP **supports** (D wrong). Parents neither sit out entirely (B) nor stay the whole time (C).

## Q7 — Caregiver Care (p. 699)

Which one of the following statements about assessing caregiver needs and burdens is correct?

- A. A caregiver is defined as someone who spends more than 24 hours per week with the care recipient.
- B. Care recipients are less likely to provide reliable information when interviewed alone.
- C. Anyone who identifies as a caregiver should be offered an assessment.
- D. There are no validated tools available for assessing caregiver burden.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Anyone who identifies as a caregiver should be offered an assessment.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> There's no fixed hour-based definition (A); care recipients are often **more** reliable when interviewed alone (B); and **validated tools exist** (e.g., the Zarit Burden Interview, D wrong).

## Q8 — Caregiver Care (p. 699)

Which one of the following aspects of care is one of the most burdensome challenges for caregivers caring for people with heart failure?

- A. Managing pain.
- B. Enforcing dietary restrictions.
- C. Lifting.
- D. Behavior disturbances.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Enforcing dietary (sodium/fluid) restrictions.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Daily monitoring and enforcing **low-sodium/fluid limits** (plus medication and weight tracking) is a major, distinctive burden in heart-failure caregiving. Lifting and behavior disturbances dominate other conditions (e.g., dementia).

## Q9 — Caregiver Care (p. 699)

Which one of the following statements about caring for a person at the end of life is most accurate?

- A. The care recipient's transfer to a long-term care facility has a positive effect on the caregiver's burden.
- B. Caregivers are most likely to need intensified support for depression after the care recipient's death.
- C. Early palliative care interventions for patients with serious illness improves quality of life.
- D. Life-prolonging interventions in the late stages of heart failure have been shown to relieve caregiver burden.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Early palliative care improves quality of life.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> Early palliative care improves patient (and caregiver) quality of life. Institutionalization often **doesn't** relieve caregiver burden (A); caregivers frequently need support **during** caregiving, with risk continuing—but the strongest evidence-backed statement is C (B overstated); aggressive life-prolonging care tends to **increase** burden (D wrong).

## Q10 — Cochrane for Clinicians: Anticoagulation for the Long-term Treatment of VTE in Patients with Cancer (p. 676)

Which one of the following statements about the prevention of recurrent venous thromboembolism (VTE) in patients with cancer is correct?

- A. Use of low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) reduces the risk of recurrent VTE to a greater extent than vitamin K antagonists.
- B. Use of LMWH reduces the risk of recurrent VTE to a greater extent than direct oral anticoagulants.
- C. Use of LMWH increases bleeding risk compared with direct oral anticoagulants.
- D. Use of LMWH increases bleeding risk compared with vitamin K antagonists.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **A — LMWH reduces recurrent VTE more than vitamin K antagonists.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> In cancer-associated VTE, LMWH outperformed warfarin for recurrence (this Cochrane era). DOACs are now comparable/preferred in many cases — LMWH is **not** superior to DOACs (B) and DOACs carry **more GI bleeding** (so C, D as worded are wrong).

## Q11 — Cochrane for Clinicians: Alternative Interventions for Chronic Prostatitis/Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome in Men (p. 677)

Which one of the following statements about nonpharmacologic therapies for men with long-standing pelvic pain and urinary symptoms is correct?

- A. Transrectal thermotherapy and physical therapy are likely to be clinically beneficial.
- B. Extracorporeal shock wave therapy and acupuncture are likely to be clinically beneficial.
- C. Prostatic massage improves prostatitis symptoms.
- D. There is high-quality evidence for the effectiveness of lifestyle modifications for urinary symptoms.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **B — Extracorporeal shock wave therapy and acupuncture are likely beneficial.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> The review found ESWT and acupuncture probably help chronic prostatitis/CPPS. Evidence is lacking for prostatic massage (C) and lifestyle modification (low-quality, D); thermotherapy/PT weren't clearly beneficial (A).

## Q12 — Practice Guidelines: Cancer Screening — ACS Annual Summary (p. 719)

Which one of the following statements about the 2018 cancer screening recommendations from the American Cancer Society (ACS) is correct?

- A. Colorectal cancer screening should be offered at age 40 in average-risk patients.
- B. Lung cancer screening in high-risk patients should occur biannually after shared decision-making.
- C. Prostate cancer screening can be done at age 50 in average-risk patients who opt for testing after a physician-initiated discussion.
- D. Cervical cancer screening should occur before age 21 in those with early onset sexual activity.

> [!success]- Reveal answer
> **C — Prostate screening may begin at 50 in average-risk men who choose it after an informed discussion.** ✅ Official-verified · Confidence: High
> ACS starts CRC screening at **45** (not 40, A); lung cancer screening is **annual** low-dose CT (B wrong); cervical screening starts at **21 regardless of sexual history** (D wrong).
