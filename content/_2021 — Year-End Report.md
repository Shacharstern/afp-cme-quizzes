## High-yield clusters for Shlav A

The year skewed heavily toward **women's health / OB-gyn, screening guidelines, and MSK** — all board-dense.

**Screening & prevention (USPSTF-heavy).** HCV 18–79, HIV 15–65, HBV in high-risk, pediatric HTN (obesity = key risk), carotid stenosis (D rec), dyslipidemia VA/DoD (moderate statin for DM), colonoscopy intervals (≥10 adenomas → 1 yr), breast cancer (BSE **not** recommended; 40s = individualized; chest-radiation survivor → mammography at 30).

**Women's health / OB.** Hormonal contraception (no Pap needed to start; postplacental copper IUD; POPs lack estrogen's VTE risk), labor dystocia (confirm contraction adequacy with IUPC before arrest; immediate pushing; delayed admission in latent labor), pain in labor (continuous support ↑ SVD), mifepristone/misoprostol regimen, active 3rd-stage management, chronic pelvic pain → pelvic floor PT, folic acid for any patient who can become pregnant.

**MSK / sports.** Upper-extremity nerve entrapments (long thoracic → winging; ulnar/Froment; AIN/OK sign), radius/ulna fractures (radial head fat-pad sign; distal radius → median nerve), hip pain (FAI with +FADIR/FABER; GTPS; deep gluteal syndrome), telemedicine MSK exam.

**Psych / behavioral.** Bipolar (depression ≈ unipolar; baseline lipids/A1C; relapse signs), suicide (lithium reduces risk; women attempt-men die; outpatient = ideation without plan), eating disorders (anorexia → family-based therapy; BMP first), motivational interviewing, treatment-resistant depression (only atypical-antipsychotic augmentation works).

**Other recurring pearls.** Difficult asthma (confirm dx first; eosinophilia → biologics), topical steroids (Cushing risk; FTU rule), CSF analysis (Listeria in elderly/immunocompromised; SAH LP if >12 h + equivocal CT), frailty (weight loss = late stage; prefrail reversible), COVID antigen tests (high specificity, prevalence drives false-positives), ATS smoking cessation (varenicline first even with psych comorbidity), OSA (AHI ≥15), testosterone (only small sexual-function benefit).
