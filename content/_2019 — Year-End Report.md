## High-yield clusters for Shlav A

2019 leaned **MSK/sports medicine, women's health, pediatrics, and behavioral medicine**, with strong guideline content.

**MSK / sports.** Plantar fasciitis (medial calcaneal tenderness; self-limited; PF-specific stretching), patellofemoral pain (hip + quadriceps strengthening; kinesiotaping; passive knee extension for hamstrings), adhesive capsulitis (clinical dx; oral steroids highest-risk), tendinopathy (degenerative; scapular stabilization for rotator cuff), apophysitis (Sever → squeeze test; night pain = red flag), concussion (headache most common; SCAT5; graded return), heat illness (cold-water immersion for heat stroke).

**Women's health / OB.** First-trimester bleeding (β-hCG kinetics; ectopic management; methotrexate contraindications), abnormal uterine bleeding (LNG-IUS most effective; sample ≥45/at-risk), cervical cancer screening (start 21; 5-yr HPV interval), HPV vaccine (best in HPV-naïve adolescents), perinatal depression (counseling for at-risk), gestational hypertension/preeclampsia (aspirin for high-risk; permissive BP target), interpregnancy care, hirsutism (PCOS; OCPs first-line).

**Pediatrics.** Gastroenteritis (dilute apple juice; ondansetron; rotavirus by 8 months), febrile seizures (no routine testing; complex = focal/prolonged/recurrent), amblyopia (acuity <20/40 at 3–5; atropine), depression screening (≥12), toilet training, child passenger safety (rear-facing by size), maintenance IV fluids (isotonic), infantile hemangioma (beard-area = airway risk), inborn errors of metabolism (stop feeds + IV glucose when decompensating).

**Behavioral / preventive.** Exercise/yoga/meditation for depression-anxiety, adolescent substance use (alcohol most common; rising opioid poisoning), opioid use disorder (buprenorphine timing; methadone via OTP), PTSD (trauma-focused therapy + SSRIs/SNRIs), military veteran care, urine drug testing (confirm with GC/LC-MS; poppy seeds = true positive; spiking → no metabolites), deprescribing BZRAs/statins, caregiver care.

**Other recurring pearls.** AF anticoagulation (DOAC ≈ warfarin in CKD; ASH prefers V/Q for intermediate PE), cirrhosis (6-monthly HCC ultrasound; elastography), diabetic kidney disease (confirm microalbuminuria ×2–3; death > ESRD risk), MI universal definition (MINOCA; troponin thresholds 5×/10×), hepatitis B (screen all pregnant women; entecavir/tenofovir first-line), renal cell carcinoma (incidental; CT urography for hematuria), hypothermia/frostbite (avalanche/K⁺ rules; ibuprofen; don't rewarm if refreeze risk), influenza vaccination (all ≥6 months; two doses first-time peds).
