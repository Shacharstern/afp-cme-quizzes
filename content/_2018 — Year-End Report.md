## High-yield clusters for Shlav A

2018 was a **broad primary-care year** strong in preventive guidelines (USPSTF), geriatrics, MSK, derm, and oncology.

**Preventive / USPSTF (recurring exam fodder).** Against ovarian cancer screening (D rec), against ECG screening in low-risk adults (D), prostate screening shared decision (55–69, C), skin cancer counseling for fair-skinned youth, AAA / osteoporosis risk-tool screening, depression screening systems, behavioral weight-loss referral, falls prevention with **exercise**, hepatitis B (screen all pregnant women; monitor chronic HBV for HCC).

**Geriatrics.** Geriatric assessment (prior-fall question screens fall risk; ask about weight loss; whispered-voice test), preoperative assessment (delirium risk, malnutrition → dehiscence, hearing aids/glasses reduce delirium), deprescribing antipsychotics (stop immediately for insomnia) and BZRAs, capacity assessment (Aid to Capacity Evaluation; the 4 components).

**MSK / derm.** Braces/splints (patellar brace alignment; wrist splints), bone cancer (plain films first; Ewing radiosensitive; older age poor prognosis), plantar fasciitis (OTC inserts ≈ custom), annular lesions (psoriasis Auspitz; herald patch = pityriasis rosea; nummular eczema mimics tinea), tinea pedis (terbinafine), hyperhidrosis (aluminum chloride first; >6 months = primary), hallux valgus (wide toe box).

**Pediatrics.** Well-child anticipatory guidance (window locks; early peanut/egg; whole milk at 1 yr; water between meals), recurrent abdominal pain (functional → no workup; PEG for constipation; CBT), febrile-seizure–adjacent topics, toddler nutrition, type 1 & type 2 diabetes in children, conduct disorder (3 of 15 DSM-5; stimulant first with comorbid ADHD; risperidone weight gain).

**Acute / oncology / other.** Oncologic emergencies (febrile neutropenia from chemo; hypercalcemia → saline + furosemide; tamponade = low voltage + electrical alternans), appendicitis (antibiotics-first for uncomplicated), intestinal obstruction (CT first; high BUN = dehydration), hemolytic anemia (low haptoglobin; PLASMIC; HUS after diarrhea), epistaxis (Kiesselbach; compress→directive→pack), anticoagulant bleeding (reversal for major/critical-site), PE rules (Wells/Geneva), procalcitonin-guided antibiotics, allergy testing (patch test for contact dermatitis; tryptase; stop antihistamines first), seasonal allergic rhinitis (intranasal corticosteroid monotherapy preferred), corticosteroids for sore throat (single 10-mg dexamethasone).
